About This Project

This project provides simple and practical cybersecurity guidance for small businesses, students and everyday internet users. It focuses on common threats and actions people can take to protect accounts, devices and important information.


Project Focus: Cybersecurity awareness, prevention and safe digital practices.

Common Cybersecurity Threats

🎣

Phishing

Fake emails, SMS or social-media messages designed to steal passwords, money or personal information.

🦠

Malware

Malicious software that can damage devices, spy on users or steal information.

🔑

Weak Passwords

Short, reused or predictable passwords make accounts easier to compromise.

📱

Social Engineering

Attackers manipulate people into revealing information or performing unsafe actions.

How to Protect Yourself

  • Use Strong Passwords: Use long, unique passwords for important accounts. Avoid reusing the same password everywhere.
  • Enable 2FA/MFA: Turn on two-factor or multi-factor authentication whenever the service supports it.
  • Update Devices: Install security updates for your phone, computer, browser and applications.
  • Back Up Important Data: Keep safe copies of important documents and business information so they can be recovered after an incident.

🎣 How to Spot a Phishing Message

Be careful if a message:

  • Creates urgent pressure: "Act now!"
  • Requests your password, PIN or OTP.
  • Contains a suspicious link.
  • Offers an unrealistic prize or financial opportunity.
  • Comes from an address or account you do not recognize.

Safety Rule: Do not click first. Verify the sender and the website through an official channel before taking action.

✅ Quick Security Checklist

🚨 What To Do If You Suspect an Attack

  • Stop interacting with the suspicious message or website.
  • Change the affected password from a trusted device.
  • Enable 2FA/MFA if it is not already enabled.
  • Review account activity and sign out unknown sessions.
  • Inform the relevant service provider or organization.
  • If money or sensitive information was involved, report the incident promptly to the appropriate authorities or institution.
