## ⚠️ Common Cybersecurity Threats

### 🎣 Phishing
Fake emails, SMS, websites, or social-media messages designed to steal passwords, money, or personal information.

### 🦠 Malware
Malicious software that can damage devices, spy on users, or steal information.

### 🔑 Weak Passwords
Short, reused, or predictable passwords can make accounts easier to compromise.

### 📱 Social Engineering
Attackers manipulate people into revealing information or performing unsafe actions.

## 🛡️ How to Protect Yourself

- Use strong and unique passwords.
- Enable 2FA/MFA on important accounts.
- Keep your phone, computer, browser, and apps updated.
- Back up important files.
- Avoid suspicious links and attachments.
- Never share your OTP, PIN, or password.

## 🎣 Phishing Safety

Before clicking a link:

1. Check who sent the message.
2. Look carefully at the website address.
3. Be suspicious of urgent requests.
4. Never share your password, PIN, or OTP.
5. Verify important requests through an official channel.

## ✅ Security Checklist

- [x] Strong and unique passwords
- [x] 2FA/MFA
- [x] Updated devices and applications
- [x] Safe browsing practices
- [x] Regular data backups
- [x] Protection of passwords, PINs, and OTPs

## 🚨 If You Suspect an Attack

1. Stop interacting with the suspicious message or website.
2. Change the affected password using a trusted device.
3. Enable 2FA/MFA.
4. Review account activity.
5. Sign out unknown sessions.
6. Contact the relevant service provider if necessary.
